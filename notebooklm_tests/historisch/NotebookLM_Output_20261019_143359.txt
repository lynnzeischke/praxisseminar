* **Methodological Disclaimer:** This section represents a simulated, heuristic cognitive walkthrough by artificial agent personas (low-experience employee vs. experienced user) to detect wording ambiguities and routing logic errors. It is an internal heuristic check and **must not be described as an empirical human pretest**.
* **Perspective A (Low AI Experience):** The introduction clearly defines generative KI in simple office terms. Filtering directly directs non-users past experience-dependent questions (APPLY, DETECT, COMFORT), preventing frustration. The response option "Kann ich nicht beurteilen" in Part 3 (FC) allows non-users to answer contextual questions without being forced into an ungrounded opinion.
* **Perspective B (Regular User / Commercial Specialist):** Item wordings feel realistic for modern office environments (e.g., Copilot / ChatGPT Enterprise context). The 0–10 scale for capability and 1–7 scale for agreement match standard survey expectations. Completion time is estimated at 5–7 minutes, well within the 8-minute respondent burden ceiling.
